Hi! I'm Jack, and today I'd like to tell you about my new home. Last Friday, my family moved from a small flat in the city centre to a house in a quiet neighbourhood. Moving was exhausting because we spent the whole weekend unpacking boxes and arranging furniture. Our new house has three bedrooms, a large living room and a small garden. My favourite place is my bedroom because I've finally got enough space for a desk and a bookshelf. However, I miss living near my friends. Now I have to take a bus to see them, and the journey takes about twenty minutes. Another big change is that I have more household responsibilities. I vacuum the living room twice a week, take out the rubbish every evening and sometimes water the plants. My younger sister sets the table and does the washing-up. At first, I was worried about moving, but now I'm happy here. This Saturday, we're having a housewarming party, and I can't wait to invite my friends!
